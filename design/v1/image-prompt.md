## Исходный prompt

Use case: product-mockup / stylized-concept.
Asset type: industrial design concept board for an actually buildable small DIY AI walking robot, concept only, not a manufacturing drawing.
Primary request: show an original, appealing minimalist 18–20 cm tall biped robot using SIX small hobby servos: FOUR in the Otto-style walking lower body and TWO in a camera head pan/tilt neck. No actuated knees. All views must show the SAME robot with consistent mechanical proportions.
Scene: clean warm neutral studio background, generous spacing, professional realistic industrial design presentation, not sci-fi fantasy.
Composition: one large front three-quarter hero view on the left; two smaller clear front and side views on the right. Ground shadows. Tiny small standing human hand silhouette next to side view solely for scale if it fits, otherwise omit.
Design: friendly compact rounded rectangular camera head, short two-axis gimbal neck with visible small mechanical bracket; a compact lower rounded rectangular torso; two short STRAIGHT legs without knee joints, each connected to a hip-yaw servo and a foot-roll servo; broad flat rounded rectangular feet. The feet are mechanically credible for slow waddling walking on a tabletop. NO extra joints, arms, wings, wheels or articulated fingers. Leg servo pockets and screw fasteners should be plausible. One real small camera lens centered in a horizontal dark face inset, two tiny passive circular status-light dots flanking the camera, NOT an LCD, no giant screen. No beak, no character franchise imitation. Design allows original 3D printed shells with visible subtle layer texture and screw access.
Materials: simple matte pale warm grey printed plastic shells, graphite mechanical brackets and rubber sole pads, restrained muted green accent panels only on feet. Practical split shell seams, small rear ventilation slots, rear small power switch/charging-access opening. Battery low in torso, Pi board in torso, only light camera in head.
Text: keep only short Russian labels: "КОНЦЕПТ · 6 ПРИВОДОВ", "ГОЛОВА — 2", "НОГИ — 4", "18–20 см". Use unobtrusive fine leader lines; do not show wiring/pinouts or fictitious dimensioned engineering details.
Constraints: normal buildable hobby robot, low center of mass, no bent knees, no decorative motors implying more than six actuators, consistent design across views, no logos, no watermark. The design is not a dynamic running biped.

## Уточнение при редактировании

Сохранить внешний вид и шесть приводов. Бёдра: вращение вокруг вертикальной оси; стопы: наклон вокруг продольной оси. Убрать изображения шарниров, предполагающих сгибание коленей или вращение бёдер вперёд-назад. Сохранить два привода головы и все подписи.
